# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Tarot readers and seekers who begin a reading conversationally, then move into the embedded MCP view or `/draw` web experience to shuffle, cut, choose, and reveal cards. They may be using a desktop or mobile device and need the ritual to feel private, intentional, and trustworthy without losing clarity or control.

## Product Purpose

Turn a secure, opaque tarot draw into a tactile visual ritual. The interface should make card choice feel consequential, preserve the mystery of hidden identities until confirmation, and carry the reader smoothly from intention through selection to interpretation across both Web and MCP surfaces.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, mystical, deliberate. The emotional register is a midnight Art Nouveau reading table: antique-gold craft, controlled darkness, and a sense of gathered energy. It should feel ceremonial and refined rather than theatrical for its own sake.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble neon-cyber occult dashboards, casino wins, mobile-game loot openings, generic glassmorphism, cartoon magic, or particle effects that read as confetti. Avoid slow spectacle that makes the reader wait, random motion without state meaning, and decorative UI that competes with the illustrated deck.

## Design Principles

1. **Make the choice feel consequential.** Concentrate the strongest choreography on shuffling, choosing, and revealing—the moments where user intent changes the reading.
2. **Let the deck remain the artifact.** Interface effects should frame and illuminate the original artwork, never overpower or imitate it.
3. **Build tension, then resolve cleanly.** Use controlled anticipation, a decisive card flight, and a quiet settling state instead of continuous visual noise.
4. **Mystery without ambiguity.** Hidden card identities can feel enigmatic; controls, selection order, progress, and reversibility must remain immediately understandable.
5. **One ritual across two surfaces.** Preserve the same narrative and interaction quality in Web and MCP, scaling effect density rather than changing the experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and interaction behavior. Preserve complete keyboard operation, visible focus, screen-reader stage and status updates, touch-friendly targets, and meaning that never depends on color alone. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` with immediate or short crossfade alternatives that preserve every state transition and result. Keep the standard experience smooth on mobile-class hardware and inside constrained MCP embeds.
